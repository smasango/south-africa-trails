# BT New Adventure Tours website

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive multi-page tourism website that extends the supplied logo’s South African colour palette and prominently features the real logo and tour photographs.

## Pages and navigation
- Home: photo-led introduction, featured experiences, services overview, tailored-tour journey planner, safari, transfers, conference travel, and enquiry prompts.
- About Us: factual company positioning and complete service overview without invented history or claims.
- Services: all 10 supplied services with concise descriptions and enquiry links.
- Tours: example tailored experiences for Johannesburg and Soweto, Cape Town, Kruger, Pretoria, Cradle of Humankind, and Panorama/Mpumalanga.
- Attractions: all supplied destinations, correctly named and region-labelled, including Victoria Falls as Southern Africa rather than South Africa.
- Gallery: responsive masonry-style presentation of the supplied real tour photographs with keyboard-accessible lightbox viewing.
- Request a Quote: the full tailored-tour enquiry form and clear success state.
- Contact: supplied business details, enquiry form, and direct phone, email, and WhatsApp actions.
- Privacy, terms, and cookie pages containing clearly editable, non-invented starter wording.

## Brand and visual direction
- Use the actual supplied logo at generous scale in the header and key brand moments, without changing its proportions.
- Derive the palette from the logo: deep navy, South African green, warm gold, and confident red on bright neutral surfaces.
- Use bold, clean typography, strong editorial photography, restrained motion, crisp cards, and clear calls to action.
- Prioritise the supplied photographs as authentic proof of previous tours; use them without making unsupported attraction claims.
- Create a favicon from the supplied logo and preserve a high-quality, responsive presentation across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Content and interactions
- Centralise the company name, taglines, phone, email, website, and WhatsApp link in one configuration module.
- Build reusable navigation, footer, page-heading, card, gallery, and form elements.
- Add a mobile menu, floating WhatsApp action, functional internal navigation, mail/phone links, and form validation.
- Keep forms frontend-only and show the requested confirmation message; deployment documentation will explain how to connect a live form receiver.
- Add accessible labels, focus states, descriptive image text, keyboard support, and reduced-motion handling.

## Search and sharing
- Give every public page unique titles, descriptions, Open Graph details, canonical links, and appropriate heading structure.
- Add factually supported structured data, an updated robots file, and a sitemap using the supplied live domain.

## Delivery and verification
- Update README and add detailed Truehost deployment instructions covering domain, DNS, SSL, upload, forms, and final checks.
- Record image/source guidance and editing instructions for business details, tours, attractions, and photographs.
- Verify the central flows and layouts in the live preview at desktop and mobile sizes, check links/forms/gallery, and review diagnostics.
- Package a clean, GitHub-ready source ZIP without dependencies, generated build files, Git metadata, or secrets, and inspect its full contents before delivery.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind structure required by this project.
- Store uploaded site photographs through the project asset system; keep the favicon as a small local public file.
- Use semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet and shared data modules for services, experiences, and attractions.
- The ZIP will contain the complete source project and instructions for building the deployable output; it will not include dependencies or private credentials.
